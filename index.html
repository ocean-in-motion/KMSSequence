Questions

Before you reach out.

How far in advance should I book?

For photography and videography, two to four weeks gives us room to plan properly. Editing and design work can often start sooner, depending on our current schedule.

Do you work outside your home city?

Yes. Travel is quoted separately based on distance and how many days the job needs on location.

How many revisions are included?

Every project includes a first proof and one full round of revisions. Additional rounds are available and billed at an hourly rate.

What files do I actually receive?

You get final, delivery-ready files in whatever formats you need, plus the option to request source or raw files for an additional fee.

Questions

About the photography.

How many images will I get?

It depends on the session length and type, but most half-day shoots deliver 30–60 finished images. We'll agree a rough number before the shoot.

Can you match our existing brand photos?

Yes. Send over a few reference images and we'll match lighting, color, and framing as closely as the location allows.

Do you shoot on location or in a studio?

Both. We can recommend a studio for product and portrait work, or shoot on location for lifestyle and event coverage.

Questions

About the videography.

How long does a project usually take?

A short brand film typically runs two to four weeks from treatment to final delivery, depending on how many shoot days and revision rounds it needs.

Do you handle sound and music too?

Yes, dialogue cleanup, mixing, and licensed music are part of the editing pass, not a separate add-on.

What formats do I receive the final film in?

You'll get a master file plus common exports for web, social, and any specific aspect ratios your platforms need.

About the editing.

Can you edit footage or photos we shot ourselves?

Yes. Send over your raw files and any reference looks, and we'll quote based on volume and the amount of cleanup needed.
